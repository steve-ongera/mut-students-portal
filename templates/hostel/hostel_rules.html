Hostel Rules & Regulations

General Rules
  1. All students must observe hostel curfew hours
  2. Visitors must be registered at the security desk
  3. Smoking and alcohol are strictly prohibited
  4. Students must maintain cleanliness in their rooms
  5. Noise levels must be kept to a minimum after 10 PM
  6. Students are responsible for any damage to hostel property
  7. Regular room inspections will be conducted
  8. Pets are not allowed in the hostel
Visitor Policy
  • All visitors must sign in at security
  • Visitors are allowed between 8 AM and 8 PM only
  • Overnight guests are not permitted
  • Students are responsible for their visitors' conduct
Safety & Security
  • Keep your room locked at all times
  • Report any suspicious activity to security immediately
  • Do not share your room key with anyone
  • Familiarize yourself with emergency exits
  • Participate in fire drills when conducted
