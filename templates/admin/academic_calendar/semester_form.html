Semester Guidelines
Naming Convention

Use descriptive names like "Spring 2024", "Fall 2024", or "Semester 1 2024/2025".

Date Ranges

Ensure registration period falls within the semester period. Typically 2-4 weeks before semester starts.

Current Semester

Only one semester can be marked as "Current". Setting this will update all related academic activities.

Active Status

Only active semesters will be available for student enrollment and unit registration.

Important Notes

Ensure dates don't overlap with other semesters in the same academic year.
